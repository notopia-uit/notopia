Provide the external database secret before installing.
The pre-install/pre-upgrade seed hook does not replace the mandatory external
seed or maintenance procedure. Policy is loaded only at startup; the hook does
not prevent mixed-version policy writers or make authorization fail closed.
Follow the chart README before every install, upgrade, or rollback.
After failure, keep traffic blocked and controllers suspended until manual
version-matched recovery; Helm automatic rollback is unsafe for shared policy.
The retained hook service account must be removed manually after uninstall.
