**Architecture:** Reuse service chart and common-library patterns. Add dedicated persistent standalone Valkey, service principals, and Gateway API routing with an explicit streaming timeout.

**Tech Stack:** Helm, Flux, Gateway API, Istio, Nx, Node tests.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-ai-helm-design.md`

## Constraints and review focus

- No database, CNPG, Kafka, live cluster access, deployment, real secrets, or commits.
- Keep unprovisioned secrets absent from resources; do not touch review-only secrets or `.env.local`.
- Ensure startup/liveness do not depend on Redis; readiness does.
- Ensure authenticated `/ai` traffic and gRPC principals are narrowly scoped.
- Validate timeouts against pinned schemas and document upstream limitations.
- AI and Valkey releases remain suspended; release tag updates affect only selected values.

## Security review follow-up

Remove AI from the note, authorization and billing broad principal lists and add separate additive workload-selected homelab policies for AI on gRPC ports 18081, 18089 and 18084 only.
